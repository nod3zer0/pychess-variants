# Sun Tzu

Sun Tzu Chess is a dark chess variant invented by John Kipling Lewis in 2005, combining Fog of War, Crazyhouse-style drops, and Double Fischer Random Chess.

## Rules

- **Fog of War**: a player does not see the entire board – only their own pieces and the squares that they can legally move to. As in Fog of War chess, there are no checks; the game ends when a king is captured.
- **Drops**: captured pieces go to your hand and can be dropped back onto the board, as in Crazyhouse. A drop is allowed on any empty square, including ones you cannot currently see. Holding a piece to drop therefore reveals every empty square to you, since you could legally drop on any of them – a real tactical advantage, even though you still don't know what's on the squares you can't reach.
- **Double Fischer Random setup**: pawns start on their usual squares, but the remaining pieces are placed randomly on the back rank, with the king between the two rooks and the bishops on opposite-colored squares – independently for each side, so White's and Black's back ranks are not mirror images of each other.

For the closely related variant where drops are restricted to squares you can already see, see Lao Tzu.
